# IGCSE Biology 0610 Practice — illustrated explanations

Questions 555–754 now each include a subject illustration in SVG and a credited online supporting image, following the illustrated style of Question 453. The SVGs draw cells, organs, chromosomes, molecules, plants, experiments, graphs and genetic crosses, rather than text-card summaries. All 200 diagrams have been replaced with these illustrations.

There are 200 online-image placements using 74 relevant source images; related questions may reuse an image with a question-specific caption. 18 newly downloaded images fill topic gaps such as teeth, meiosis, breathing, placenta, kidneys, pollen and water cycle. Online images are bundled locally for offline use, with source, author, licence and full-size links in every caption. Supporting examples are distinguished from the actual specimens and data in each past-paper question.

The collection remains 754 Paper 2 MCQs and 13 Paper 4 papers. All question stems, choices, published keys, original paper codes/numbers and written reasoning are preserved. The earlier 554 questions and their diagrams are unchanged. The previous source corrections in Q596, Q644, Q657 and Q736 are retained. Native clickable answer tables, chapter/paper filters, four-column navigation and both Next buttons remain.

The illustrations have descriptive alt text, full-size links, local scrolling and text-size support. Supporting images fit the page and keep their original aspect ratio.
