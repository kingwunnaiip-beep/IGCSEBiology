# IGCSE Biology 0610 Practice

This release contains **554 Paper 2 questions** and **13 Paper 4 papers**: February/March 2026 (42), May/June 2026 (41–43), October/November 2025 (41–43), May/June 2025 (41–43), and October/November 2024 (41–43). The latest batch adds three complete October/November 2024 papers: 18 main questions and 96 parts worth 240 marks. Across all papers there are **427 assessable parts worth 1,037 marks**. Each paper lasts 75 minutes with a printed maximum of 80 marks. The official May/June 2026 Paper 43 scheme withdraws Question 5(a), originally worth three marks, so that component has 77 assessable marks; the withdrawn part remains reference-only.

## Install

For the combined 554-question update, install over the 504-question version. For the latest October/November 2024 three-paper update, install over the existing ten-paper release. Replace all supplied files and merge the new images into your existing images/ folder. Keep your existing styles.css and all earlier images, including user uploads. Hard-refresh the browser after installing. Open index.html for Paper 2 or paper4.html for Paper 4; the header dropdown on both pages switches practice sections.

## Paper 4

Paper 43 question 5(a) is explicitly withdrawn on page 13 of its official mark scheme. It has no answer field, reveal or score control and is excluded from assessed-part and mark totals. The original 80-mark maximum and 77-mark practice total are both shown.

Choose a component from the Past paper selector, or choose All past papers to practise across every included paper. The combined view groups original question numbers by paper and follows the chapter filter across all included papers. Its totals cover all included papers. Each paper retains its own saved answers and marks in either view.

Write answers and working before revealing the mark scheme. Tick the relevant statements where requested. Use paper for drawings and record notes in the answer box. Reveal each part’s criteria and guidance, then award your own mark. This is self-marking, not automatic grading. Answers, selected statements, revealed criteria and marks are saved in this browser on this device when browser storage is available. Editing an answer clears its previous mark. Switching chapters or returning to the first question preserves your work.

Original question numbers, marks, figures and direct links to the official question paper and mark scheme are included. The chapter filter groups mixed-topic questions in every relevant chapter. Use Hide side panel to expand the question area, then Show side panel to restore the controls without changing your answers or marks. The paper is reproduced as practice parts rather than a timed exam interface. For a question requiring a measurement from the printed image, use the original PDF at its intended print size.
